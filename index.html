Vision

Vision is a personal assistant app that runs on its owner's Mac. It answers its owner's questions by voice or text: the weather, messages, email, the calendar and everyday tasks.

Vision is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and there is nothing to sign up for or download here.

With the owner's permission, Vision can read the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar, read-only, so it can answer questions such as "Any new email?" or "What's on today?". It never sends, changes or deletes anything in Google.
